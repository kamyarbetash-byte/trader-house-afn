# Raahbar — USD/AFN Live

Live candlestick chart of the dollar-to-afghani open-market rate by province (Kabul Sarai Shahzada, Mazar-i-Sharif, Herat, Kandahar, plus Nangarhar and Ghazni), with a market-average series, drawing tools and indicators. By Raahbar (راهبر).

- **Data sources:** public Telegram channels listed in `CHANNELS` in `src/index.js` (currently [@kabul_dollar](https://t.me/kabul_dollar), [@Mazar_Nerkh](https://t.me/Mazar_Nerkh), [@Exchange_Rate](https://t.me/Exchange_Rate) — Sarai Shahzada plus every province and the daily closing summary of all bid markets — and [@herat_gold](https://t.me/herat_gold), whose live-edited price board is sampled every minute), read once a minute from their web previews. Adding a channel there backfills ~45 days automatically.
- **Runs on:** a Cloudflare Worker with a cron trigger (every minute) and a D1 database. Each city's history is kept in one `snap` row so a page load costs a handful of row reads.
- **News:** Bing News searches and Afghan outlet feeds (Amu TV, Ariana News, KabulNow, Hasht-e Subh), filtered for Da Afghanistan Bank / afghani topics and checked every 30 min (Google News blocks Workers; DAB and Pajhwok publish no feeds) plus Telegram posts that mention an auction (لیلام), bid (بولی) or the central bank.
- **Endpoints:** `/` chart page · `/api/history` all city series · `/api/updates?since=<unix>` recent ticks · `/api/news` latest news.
- **بازار بولی board:** closing buy/sell of every market from the latest all-markets summary post.
- **Demo trading (MetaTrader-style):** each viewer creates a practice account in dollars with a balance they choose (1,000–100,000). One-click SELL / volume / BUY on the chart for every market (dollar markets in $, world gold in ounces, 18K in grams), plus an order ticket (⋯) for market, buy/sell limit and buy/sell stop orders with stop loss and take profit. Positions, SL, TP and pending orders are price lines that can be dragged on the chart (dragging an entry line creates SL or TP). Pending orders and SL/TP are checked against every price since the last check; leverage 1:20 with stop-out at 50% margin level. A small terminal under the chart shows balance, equity, free margin, floating P&L, open positions, orders and history.
- **Saraf calculator:** USD ⇄ AFN at the live buy/mid/sell rate (or a typed rate), with the result written out in Dari words.
- **Gold:** real world-gold candles (5m/1h/1D) from Yahoo Finance's chart API (`XAUUSD=X`, falling back to `GC=F`), stored in D1 meta and served at `/api/xau`, extended live by the @herat_gold ounce; an 18K AFN-per-gram chart (`g18`) derived from the ounce and the Kabul dollar at each bar's time; Herat melted 18k shown in the panel; karat table (24/22/21/18/15/14) per gram and misqal at the Kabul dollar rate; jewelry calculator (gram/misqal/tola, karat, making charge in USD per gram) with the total in words.
- **Dates:** Afghan solar calendar (حمل … حوت) with the Gregorian date beside it.
- **News:** each panel shows the 5 latest; `#news-usd` / `#news-gold` open the full list.
- **Two sections:** `#usd` (dollar/afghani: provinces, bid markets, chart, saraf calculator, DAB news) and `#gold` (world ounce, karat table, gold chart, jewelry calculator, gold news). The chart moves between them.
- **Indicators menu:** SMA ×2, EMA ×2, WMA, Bollinger, Envelopes, Keltner, Donchian, Parabolic SAR on the chart; RSI, MACD, Stochastic, CCI, ATR, Williams %R, Momentum in their own panes; every parameter editable.
- **Live updates:** world gold refreshes every 15 s from `/api/live` (Yahoo 1-minute bars, shared 10 s cache) and moves the last candle, the karat table and open positions; dollar markets poll `/api/updates` every 15 s.
- **Search engines:** `/` and `/gold` are rendered with today's rates in the title, description, a visible summary table and FAQ structured data; `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `/logo.jpg`.
- **Chart tools (TradingView-style rail):** trend, ray, horizontal line, horizontal ray, vertical line, rectangle, Fibonacci, measure, text; click-click or drag; magnet to OHLC; stay-in-tool; hide; select, move and reshape drawings; undo/delete/clear (saved per viewer). Zoom in/out, fit, tall chart, full screen. Timeframes 5m–1D, deals-only.
- **Accounts:** `src/auth.js` — email + password sign-up and login (PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes with per-user salt), random session tokens in an HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite=Lax cookie (only their SHA-256 is stored), per-IP rate limits, same-origin checks. Each account stores its chart drawings and demo trading account (`/api/user/data`), so analyses follow the user across devices. Email verification and password reset send through Resend once D1 `meta` holds `resendKey` and `mailFrom`. The owner sees accounts at `/admin?key=…` (CSV at `/admin.csv?key=…`); the key is in D1 `meta.adminKey`, not in the repo. Earlier sign-ups without a password stay in the `members` table.
- **Security:** every response carries security headers (CSP allowing only this site and the pinned chart library, no framing, nosniff, HSTS, referrer and permissions policies). `/api/join` accepts only same-origin, small JSON posts. All SQL is parameterized; page text is set with textContent. `/robots.txt` hides `/admin` and `/api/`.

Public address: **https://nirkh.com** (Workers custom domains in `wrangler.toml`; www and the workers.dev link redirect there, `/api/` excepted).

Deploys automatically to Cloudflare on every push to `main` (Workers Builds).
